# Symmetries & Rotations

## Rotational Symmetry (90° Clockwise)

For an $N \times N$ grid, rotating it by 90 degrees clockwise maps each cell $(i, j)$ to:
$$(i, j) \to (j, N - 1 - i)$$

## Mirror Symmetry (Left to Right)

For an $N \times N$ grid, reflecting it horizontally (left to right mirror) maps each cell $(i, j)$ to:
$$(i, j) \to (i, N - 1 - j)$$
